Call The Exterminator is an upcoming game by [KunuStudios](https://kunustudios.itch.io/). It's being developed in [EFPSE](https://www.easyfpshub.com/) so stumbled on the dev's YouTube videos while learning about the engine.

Kunu has put up a particularly cool [tutorial video on weapon animations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ad6ZQPrfcec). The result is pretty damn impressive and that's reflected in his game.

Definitely Wishlisted and I'll try to have a go at the demo soon.

Go watch [Mikulu Games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5hEztkta5o) play the demo.
