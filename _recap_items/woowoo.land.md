I read [Berserker Magazine](https://www.breakdownpress.com/store/berserker-1) which had an interview with [Joey Holder](https://www.joeyholder.com/) - an artist and designer. 

I checked out their work and found [THE WOOSPHERE](https://www.woowoo.land/) - an awesome interactive web experience about interacting with aliens from the future. The design is sick and it's surprisingly mobile responsive.

[Florian Mecklenburg](https://florianmecklenburg.com/) is credited with graphic design/direction.
