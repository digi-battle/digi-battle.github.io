Absolutely love this site and the artwork. [e.biscuit1133](https://www.instagram.com/e.biscuit1133/) creates these mixed-media collages using trading cards and showcases them on this site. 

The work really appeals to me and the site is very cleverly designed (desktop and mobile). Definitely go check it out.
