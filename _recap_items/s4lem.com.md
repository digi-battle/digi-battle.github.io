The band SALEM are very *in* right now and I know next to nothing about them (but their [Gucci Mane Round One Remix](https://soundcloud.com/salem-archive/gucci-mane-round-one-gucci-mane) is hard). 

Their website is very up my street. Definitely brutalist in style, and similar to Yung Lean's [World Affairs website](/blog/2026/02/28/recap-february-2026#website-world-affairs). The main site is styled after what I assume is a police booking finger-print form. The [Shopify store](https://s4lem.myshopify.com/) loses the theming but keeps the black+white grid layout. 

I really love how blank the pages are while still filling the viewport. My favourite thing is the crossed out products on the store that are out of stock.
