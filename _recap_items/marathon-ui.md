I saw someone playing Classic Marathon at Play Expo Glasgow and was transfixed. Not by the gameplay, which seems like a typical 90's Doom-clone, but by the interface.

The gameplay is embedded within this machine that displays your vitals, equipment, radar etc. It looks fantastic, especially on a 4:3 display.

Suspect this may have provided some inspiration for [Kigot Design Studio's Website](/blog/2026/03/31/recap-march-2026#website-kigot-design-studio).
