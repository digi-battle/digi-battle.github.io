I'm mad late to this party. [wheresgeorge.com](https://www.wheresgeorge.com/) has been tracking the circulation of U.S. currency since 1998 and lets you track where your notes have been and where they go. 

A really nice community project. 

The site itself oozes early 00's charm, the design is credited to [Royce Redfern](https://redfearn.co/).
