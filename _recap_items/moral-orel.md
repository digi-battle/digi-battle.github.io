Meant to talk about this last month, but I watched all of Morel Orel on [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/moral-orel-complete-720p/1-01+-+The+lords+greatest+gift.mp4).

It's a stop-motion black comedy that aired on Adult Swim. It follows Orel, a naive and happy-go-lucky Protestant boy, as he navigates life. The show's cynical and witty and wickedly funny. 

The animation is charming and juxtaposes the often harrowing themes of the show. 

I honestly loved it, especially the final season.
