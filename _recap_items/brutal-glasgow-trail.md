Glasgow City Heritage Trust hosted an *interactive multimedia exhibition* exploring Brutalist architecture in Glasgow. 

I took my son on the trail and saw 5 of the 8 stops, but unfortunately none of them had any of the stamp books left. It was a nice day out and I came home with a new appreciation of buildings that I've walked past 100 times without noticing, like The Savoy Centre.

And clocked in over 18,000 steps!

Possibly my favourite building in Glasgow was on the route and now I know it's name: [The BOAC](https://www.glasgowheritage.org.uk/brutal-glasgow-boac/).
