I played the game Metal Garden on PC - it's sick. 

Made by [tinerasoft](https://tinerasoft.net/), a single developer from Croatia, Metal Garden is a short, atmospheric singleplayer FPS game about exploring a megastructure. 

It's very successful in a lot of ways, particularly the movement, atmosphere and level design. The fact that it was made by one person is honestly incredible. Since playing it I've been inspired to play more new games and put some thought to my own game projects.

There's an update coming soon that will include developer commentary so I'll definitely be replaying for that.

Everyone should pay the &pound;5 cost and 2.5 hour time investment to experience this game.
