    Intro

    This month I have been distracted by video games!

    Started out by playing Final Fantasy 8, which is pretty good so far. In terms of technology it definitely feels like the natural next step from FF7. It's fun and has been scratching that JRPG itch.

    But I did put it down after a week to start playing around in the game engine EFPSE, and ended up making a clone of Spooky's Jump Scare Mansion. I finished the game and put the build and source up on GitHub.

    It was a lot of fun and honestly the most excited I've been about a project in a good while.

    Making another game might be my next project.

    HOWEVER - I have been ignoring my card site redesign so that is now the priority again. Still a handful of annoying outstanding items before I can think about go-live, but it's getting close.
